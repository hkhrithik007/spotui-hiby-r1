# Support

This project is maintained by one person as a community research/modding project.

Issues, questions, and pull requests are welcome, but responses are not guaranteed. I may not always have the time, hardware, or capacity to troubleshoot individual devices.

Use the repository's structured issue forms for bug reports, beta-test
results, and substantial change proposals. Reports that omit required device,
version, reproduction, safety, or privacy information may be closed until the
missing details are available.

Substantial implementation work requires prior maintainer agreement. Opening
an issue or pull request does not create a support obligation, reserve review
time, imply approval, or guarantee that a change will be merged. See
`CONTRIBUTING.md` for the current contribution policy.

Optional donations may support test hardware, documentation, maintenance, and continued experimentation. Donations do not purchase firmware, Spotify access, device support, warranty, priority fixes, private builds, or guaranteed features.

Nothing in this project is paywalled.
